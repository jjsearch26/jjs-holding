Privacy & Data Promise

Your information deserves careful, responsible handling.

Jonathan James Search (“JJS”) is committed to protecting the privacy of clients, candidates and professional contacts and to using personal information only for legitimate recruitment, advisory and business purposes.

Our approach

This page explains the kinds of personal information JJS may collect, how that information is used and how we protect it. We aim to collect only information that is relevant to our relationship with you.

What kind of personal information we collect

Depending on how you interact with JJS, we may collect:

  • contact information;
  • professional and career information;
  • business and client relationship information;
  • information you choose to provide through inquiries, forms, email, telephone calls or meetings; and
  • limited technical information needed to operate and secure our website and communications.

How personal information is used

JJS may use personal information to communicate with you, respond to inquiries, support recruitment and executive search activity, assess relevant opportunities, deliver client services, maintain appropriate business records, operate our website and communications, and meet applicable legal or contractual obligations.

Who personal information is shared with

JJS does not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Personal information is not provided to third parties except where you specifically ask or authorize JJS to do so in connection with a recruitment, search or advisory process, where a service provider must process information solely on JJS's behalf to operate our website or communications, or where disclosure is required by law.

SMS privacy commitment

SMS consent is not shared with third parties.

Data retention & security

JJS retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for appropriate business or recruitment recordkeeping, or where required by law or contract.

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

Your choices

You may ask JJS to review, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable legal and business recordkeeping requirements. You may also ask us to update your communication preferences.

Questions about your information?

Please contact Jonathan James Search through the website inquiry form.
